Which Idol would answer your prayer?

Seven of the Synod bound their Auras into the world, and the faithful still pray to them. Answer thirty-nine questions and find the one whose nature is closest to yours.

With hints on, answers glow in their idols' colours as you hover them, and each idol's mark flares when you commit. With hints off, you answer blind. You can switch this at any point. Pick an answer, then press Next to move on.
